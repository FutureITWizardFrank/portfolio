Plant monitor

A microcontroller in a plant pot that tells me when the soil is dry. It's the smallest device on my network, and it uses the same DNS, HTTPS and notification setup as everything else.

Board
ESP8266, programmed in the Arduino IDE
Sensor
Analog soil moisture probe, calibrated against dry air and water
Network
Static address, Pi-hole for DNS, HTTPS through Caddy on the home server
Alerts
ntfy push three times a day

How it works

  • On boot, it joins Wi-Fi, syncs its clock over NTP, and starts a small web server.
  • Opening its page shows the current moisture percentage and whether the plant needs water.
  • Every five minutes it takes a reading, and at the scheduled times it pushes that reading to my phone.
  • The board only speaks plain HTTP, so Caddy on the home server puts HTTPS in front of it like every other local service.
